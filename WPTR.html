Meeting Recap: Discovery Session

Escaping OneDrive Chaos & Manual AFPs

Attendees: William Farrell (WPTR Ltd) and Oliver Abrahamson (BigChange).

WPTR Ltd is currently managing extensive catering and laundry maintenance within the prison sector (95% of workload, primarily sub-contracting for Amey). Key challenges discussed:

  • Application for Payment (AFP) Nightmare: Consolidating 100-200 paper job sheets per month to match against QuickBooks invoices is highly manual and time-consuming.
  • Scattered Documentation: Relying on millions of disjointed OneDrive folders and excel spreadsheets to store scanned job sheets and site assets.
  • Disconnected Quoting: Reactive visits that require follow-on parts (e.g., a faulty gas valve) suffer delays because paper notes must be manually typed into QuickBooks quotes in the office.
  • Lack of Transparency: Managing 200-400 open jobs simultaneously across reactive, remedial, and planned installation categories is increasingly difficult without a live dashboard.

Current State vs. Future State

Transitioning from manual workflows to real-time enterprise visibility.

Current State (Manual & Disjointed)

  • Paper Job Sheets: Engineers fill out paper forms outside prisons, returning them to the office where parts/hours are manually added and photocopied.
  • AFP Bottleneck: Manually reviewing and matching hundreds of job sheets against QuickBooks to generate the Application for Payment for Amey.
  • Siloed Asset Data: Site asset details (model numbers, serials) are buried in external spreadsheets.

Future State (BigChange)

  • Digital Workflows: Engineers input job details via an easy-to-use app, instantly creating branded digital job cards ready for the client.
  • One-Click AFP Consolidation: Select completed jobs in the CRM and generate a consolidated invoice/AFP instantly.
  • Seamless Follow-On Quoting: An incomplete job (e.g., needing a part) instantly flags the office to generate a QuickBooks-style quote linked directly to the original visit.

The Business Case for WPTR Ltd

Why switching to BigChange drives immediate operational value for your growing team.

Operational Efficiency

  • Categorised Dashboards: Easily filter your 200-400 open jobs into distinct buckets: Reactive, Remedial, and Installations.
  • Site-Specific Assets: Assign catering and laundry assets directly to sites (e.g., Belmarsh) so engineers have instant access to serials and histories.

Compliance & Risk

  • Custom Worksheets: Enforce mandatory drop-downs and text entries (e.g., "Why is a gas valve needed?") ensuring complete write-ups before job closure.
  • Planned Maintenance: Automate scheduling for CP42 gas certs and annual services to recur automatically and alert the office.

Financial Integrity

  • AFP Automation: Radically reduce the time spent generating AFPs for Amey by linking job references directly to consolidated invoices.
  • Standardised Parts Library: Pre-load common parts (e.g., Rational door seals) so the office can quote with one click.
