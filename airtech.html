Meeting Recap: Discovery Session

Escaping Software Limitations

Attendees: Simon (Airtech), Oliver Abrahamson (BigChange), Lee Wilkinson (BigChange).

Airtech is currently hindered by disjointed systems (Pace and Epicore) causing severe operational leakage. Key challenges discussed:

  • £10,000+ Lost to Missing Paperwork: Hours wasted chasing electrical certs, airflow documents, and photos, resulting in un-invoiced jobs.
  • Tenant Access Disputes: 50% of fallouts are tenants refusing access. Without geo-stamped evidence or automated SMS, housing providers are placing the blame entirely on Airtech.
  • Information Silos: Engineers are sent to jobs without full survey notes, or assigned to tasks they aren't trained for (e.g., "pen doc" / boxing), leading to disgruntled customers and re-work.
  • Housing Provider Frustration: Inability to easily evidence roof tile vents or PIV installations causes compliance pushback on mold remediation.

Current State vs. Future State

Transitioning from a filing cabinet mentality to an all-in-one compliance engine.

Current State (Pace & Paper)

  • Disconnected Workflow: Surveyors use one app, the office copies and pastes notes, and engineers get partial details via email.
  • Defenseless against No-Shows: No definitive proof to show housing providers that tenants ignored calls or refused access.
  • Skill Mismatches: Scheduling errors result in engineers arriving on site unable to complete the specific ventilation install.

Future State (BigChange)

  • One Unified Portal: From survey to install, all photos, NIC certs, and airflow documents live in one instantly accessible record.
  • Ironclad Evidence: Geo-stamped photos and automated SMS trails prove Airtech's compliance and attendance instantly.
  • Intelligent Routing: The system automatically assigns the right engineer with the right skills (e.g., pen doc capabilities) to the correct job.

The Business Case for Airtech

Why switching to BigChange drives immediate operational and financial value.

Financial Recoupment

  • Stop the Leak: Eliminate the estimated £10k+ annual loss caused directly by un-invoiced jobs and missing paperwork.
  • Eliminate Fallout Costs: Reduce the 50% "no-show" fallout rate with automated day-before SMS reminders to tenants.

Compliance & Evidence

  • Mandatory Workflows: Engineers cannot complete the job until the Airflow document and photos (e.g., roof tile vents) are uploaded.
  • Housing Provider Trust: Give clients access to a dedicated portal so they can pull their own certificates, saving your office hours of admin.

Operational Ease

  • Idiot-Proof App: A simple, big-button mobile app that engineers actually want to use, featuring voice-to-text dictation.
  • Van Checks: Replace ignored paper van checks with mandatory digital fleet walkarounds to protect against vehicle damage disputes.
