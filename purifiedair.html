Meeting Recap: Scoping Call

Replacing Protean & Integrating Systems

With the recent private equity buyout and the planned sunsetting of Protean, Purified Air needs a robust Field Service Management tool that can integrate seamlessly with HubSpot and an upgraded Sage 200 system. Key challenges discussed:

  • System Silos & Errors: Historical setup errors in Protean (like conflicting customer codes) and lack of updates have created inefficiencies. You need a "single source of truth."
  • Complex Equipment & Parts Management: Servicing multi-component systems (like ESPs with various filter types and UV lamps) at locations like Nando's requires precise part identification. Currently, poor photos (e.g., zoomed-in tape measures) or vague descriptions lead to return visits.
  • Consolidated Invoicing: Clients working through portals like Access Maintain require monthly consolidated billing matching their specific job references.
  • Data Accuracy for Equity Partners: The new board will demand greater accountability, such as exact serial numbers for replaced parts, which engineers frequently miss today.
  • Contract Management: Missing contract end dates causing revenue leakage and visibility issues across the business.
  • Inefficient Routing: Repeat visits and excessive travel time caused by poor planning and disconnected scheduling tools.
  • Administrative Bottlenecks: Manual time sheet reconciliation costing up to 1 day per week of office admin on this task alone.

Current State vs. Future State

Transitioning from a sunsetting product to a dynamic, rule-based operation.

Current State (Protean & Spreadsheets)

  • Ambiguous Field Data: Engineers submit unclear photos or text, requiring the office to interpret or guess what was actually done.
  • Manual Consolidation: Reviewing written engineer notes for legibility and manually collating photos to generate client invoices.
  • Rigid Scheduling: Schedulers relying on complex, color-coded Excel spreadsheets that lack real-time geographical or inventory awareness.

Future State (BigChange)

  • Mandatory Structured Workflows: Engineers cannot complete a job without answering specific questions (e.g., logging exact serial numbers) and providing clear, dictated notes.
  • Automated Document Generation: Professional, branded photo sheets and service reports are generated instantly upon job completion.
  • Intelligent, Integrated Hub: BigChange acts as the operational core, efficiently receiving jobs from HubSpot and feeding financial data to Sage 200.
