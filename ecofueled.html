Meeting Recap: Discovery Session

Escaping the Startup Phase

Attendees: Shaun Adkins (Eco Fueled) and Oliver Abrahamson (BigChange).

Eco Fueled has outgrown its initial startup tools. Expanding across the South, you need a system that supports growth rather than stifling it. Key challenges identified:

  • The App Juggle: Operating across Monday.com, SafetyCulture (iAuditor), Excel, and WhatsApp is causing significant double-handling and wasted admin time.
  • Tracking Blindspots: A lack of vehicle tracking or location-stamped timesheets makes it impossible to verify when sub-contracted teams arrive or leave a site.
  • Multi-Day Installs: Difficulty tracking progress on 3-to-4 day Air Source Heat Pump installs; engineers often forget to take required photos before closing up panels.
  • Rural Locations: Finding remote domestic properties (like Warmer Homes grant sites) is a challenge without integrated what3words navigation.

Current State vs. Future State

Transitioning from manual workflows to real-time enterprise visibility.

Current State (Manual & Disjointed)

  • WhatsApp Dispatching: Sending job details the night before via text, leading to lost information and lack of auditability.
  • Unverified Hours: No concrete way to verify if teams stayed on-site to finish remedials, causing subsequent days to be sacrificed for firefighting.
  • Manual Handovers: Office staff wasting hours collating photos from WhatsApp to build handover packs.

Future State (BigChange)

  • All-In-One Hub: Replace Monday.com and SafetyCulture. Use BigChange for CRM, scheduling, and mandatory digital workflows, while keeping Xero and Spruce.
  • Group Jobs: Engineers can "dip in" to a multi-day install daily, taking mandatory progressive photos before closing panels.
  • Accountability: Live vehicle tracking (or geo-stamped timesheets for subbies) ensures you know exactly when teams arrive and depart.
