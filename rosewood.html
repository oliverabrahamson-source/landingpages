What manual operations are costing Rosewood

Not a generic pitch — these are the specific issues raised on the call with Brad.

01

Fragmented systems, no single source of truth

SiteDocs and SmartSheets run side by side, backed up by phone calls. Nobody is clearly held accountable for an open job, and everyone uses the tools differently.

"It's just all very messy."
02

No real-time visibility of job status

There's no reliable way to see which jobs are complete, need parts, or are ready to invoice — without ringing round the team. Pulling one open-jobs report took someone five hours.

"It took someone about five hours to put all the open jobs in a spreadsheet."
03

An invoicing backlog that's quietly bleeding cash

300–400 completed jobs haven't been invoiced — against an average job value of ~£20k for a two-week job, and up to £120k–£140k on larger projects.

"There's like 300, 400 jobs still not been invoiced."
04

No formal health & safety workflow

No structured dynamic risk assessment, RAMS sign-off or vehicle check process today — while tender panels and supply chain partners are asking to see exactly that.

"Your big supply chains… they want to see that."
