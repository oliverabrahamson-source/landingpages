Meeting Recap: Discovery Session

Escaping Software Limitations

Attendees: Matt, Emma, Leanne, David (Tip Top Toilets) alongside Oliver, Dave, and Martin (BigChange & Quixo).

Tip Top Toilets is facing critical hurdles with field operations due to the inadequacy of Inspire Mobile. Key challenges discussed:

  • Defra Compliance & Waste Notes: Inspire lacks the ability to capture photos, driver inputs, and instantly generate digital Waste Transfer Notes for customers upon completion. Currently operating heavily on pen and paper.
  • Double-Handling Systems: Because Inspire Mobile is difficult for drivers to read and use, Leanne is forced to manually manage service runs through Syrinx simultaneously.
  • VPN & Connectivity Issues: Severe delays (3+ months) were caused by Inspire requiring VPNs that clashed with dynamic Starlink IPs. A true web-based, offline-capable mobile app is urgently needed.
  • Disconnected Fleet Management: Defect logging and MOT scheduling (currently handled by Jarvis) are separate from the core operational flow.

Current State vs. Future State

Transitioning from clunky driver experiences to a seamless, integrated mobile operation.

Current State (Inspire Mobile & Paper)

  • Unreliable Mobile Access: Drivers struggle with Inspire's layout and suffer connectivity lockouts due to VPN requirements.
  • Siloed Operations: Managing the office in Inspire but planning service runs in Syrinx causes major inefficiencies for Leanne.
  • Manual Compliance: Waste Transfer and Consignment notes are handled via pen and paper, risking Defra compliance issues.

Future State (BigChange & Quixo Sync)

  • Idiot-Proof Driver App: A robust, simple mobile app that works completely offline. No VPNs required.
  • Two-Way Inspire Integration: Quixo handles the background magic. Book jobs in Inspire, manage and execute flawlessly in BigChange, and send status updates right back.
  • Automated Digital Waste Notes: The moment a collection or disposal is made, a fully branded, Defra-compliant Waste Transfer Note (with photos and signatures) is emailed instantly.

The Business Case for Tip Top Toilets

Why switching the mobile and scheduling operation to BigChange drives immediate value.

Operational Consolidation

  • Retire Syrinx: Stop paying for and managing multiple scheduling systems. Handle all deliveries, collections, and service runs in one unified BigChange view.

Compliance & Risk

Financial Integrity
