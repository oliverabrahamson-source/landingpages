Meeting Recap: Discovery Session

Escaping the WhatsApp & Excel Chaos

Attendees: Kerry (Office Manager), Ben (Operations Manager), Brandon, and Oliver Abrahamson (BigChange).

Ecobright Electrical is experiencing growth but is currently restricted by manual scheduling and disjointed communication. Key challenges discussed:

  • Cyber Essentials Risk: Relying on WhatsApp for tenant data and job photos poses a security risk, potentially hindering your ability to win new social housing contracts.
  • Tracking Blindspots: Without a live PDA tracker, the office has no real-time visibility into where the 11+ engineers are or what jobs have been completed until the end of the day.
  • Callback Confusion: Struggling to identify which engineer (or subby) attended a specific job when a callback or remedial issue arises.
  • Sage 50 Delays: Manually chasing down WhatsApp photos and timesheets before manually entering invoices into Sage 50.

Current State vs. Future State

Transitioning from manual workflows to a secure, enterprise-ready portal.

Current State (Manual & Reactive)

  • Unsecured Communication: Transmitting sensitive tenant data and job reports through WhatsApp.
  • End-of-Day Data Dumps: The office must wait until the end of the day to process paperwork, causing delays in quoting and invoicing.
  • Form Friction: Missing crucial compliance checks (like the "Fridge Form" after a drop test) because there is no way to enforce completion.

Future State (BigChange)

  • Cyber Essentials Ready: A fully secure, encrypted platform that protects tenant data and satisfies the strict requirements of social housing contracts.
  • Live Drag & Drop Scheduling: Instantly view engineer locations and schedules on a live map, allowing for rapid dispatch without endless phone calls.
  • Mandatory PDA Workflows: Engineers *must* complete the "Fridge Form," attach photos, and capture tenant signatures before closing the job.

The Business Case for Ecobright

Why switching to BigChange drives immediate operational and financial value.

Scale Without Headcount

  • Save an Admin Salary: Automate the manual task of copying data from WhatsApp into Sage, allowing Kerry, Ben, and Brandon to manage more work without the MD needing to hire extra office staff.
  • Intelligent Subby Management: Seamlessly manage both direct employees and subcontractors on one platform.

Compliance & Inventory

  • Contract-Specific Van Stock: Track specific materials (e.g., fans vs. smoke alarms) dedicated to particular engineers and contracts directly from the mobile app.
  • Automated Tenant Comms: Send automated ETA text messages to social housing tenants, reducing failed access rates and fulfilling SLA requirements.

Financial Integrity

  • Live Sage 50 Integration: Send completed job data and invoices directly into your Sage 50 desktop software with zero double-entry.
  • Exact Job Costing: Track travel time, time on site, and material costs to see your exact margin on every single electrical call-out.
